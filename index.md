# Kotireddy Syamala

**Autonomous Aerial Systems · Learning-Based & Geometric Control · Robotics Perception**

I am a research-oriented robotics engineer specializing in **autonomous aerial systems**, **multimodal perception**, and **learning-based control**. I hold an M.Sc. in **Autonomous Vehicle Engineering** from the University of Naples Federico II and have conducted research at **Cranfield University (UK)** on perception and sensor fusion for GPS-denied environments.

My work focuses on building **robust, resource-aware autonomous robotic systems** that tightly integrate perception, estimation, and control, with an emphasis on **real-world deployment and experimental validation**.

---

## Research Interests

### 1. Autonomous Aerial Systems (Primary)
My primary research interest is in **autonomous UAVs operating in unstructured and GPS-denied environments**. I focus on perception-driven autonomy, safe navigation, and robust landing using multimodal sensing.

Key topics:
- UAV autonomy in degraded sensing conditions  
- Safe landing zone detection  
- Terrain understanding and altitude estimation  
- Embedded, real-time perception for aerial robots  

---

### 2. Learning-Based & Geometric Control (Secondary)
My second research focus is on combining **learning-based methods with geometric and model-based control**, aiming to preserve stability, interpretability, and real-time feasibility.

Key topics:
- Learning-augmented control  
- Model Predictive Control (MPC)  
- Sim-to-real transfer  
- Control under sensing uncertainty  

---

### 3. Embodied & Perception-Driven Intelligence
I am interested in how physical embodiment and sensing constraints shape intelligent behavior in robotic systems, particularly the interaction between perception, planning, and control.

## Research Projects

### Autonomous UAV Safe Landing Zone Detection (Master’s Thesis)
Developed a **multimodal perception framework** combining semantic segmentation, stereo depth, and simulated radar altimetry for safe UAV landing in unstructured terrain.

- Achieved **0.12 m RMSE** in altitude estimation  
- Designed for GPS-denied environments  
- Tools: ROS 2, Python, OpenCV, TensorFlow, NVIDIA Jetson  

---

### 5G SkyRover – UAV–UGV Collaborative Autonomous System
Designed ROS 2 pipelines enabling **UAV–UGV cooperation** with low-latency communication.

- 30–50 Hz telemetry, <150 ms video latency  
- YOLO-based real-time target tracking  
- Multi-robot coordination and sensor fusion  

---

### LiDAR + RGB Dual-Sensor Detection System
Implemented long-range detection using **LiDAR clustering fused with vision**.

- RANSAC + Euclidean clustering + YOLO  
- >96% precision and recall  
- Applicable to aerial and ground robots  

---

### Model Predictive Control for Autonomous Vehicles
Designed and validated MPC controllers for speed tracking and trajectory following.

- Simulation-based evaluation  
- Applied to autonomous navigation scenarios
